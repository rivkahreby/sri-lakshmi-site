## Checking New Bookings (Admin Panel)

1. Open this link (save it as a bookmark, don't share this one publicly):
   **[your admin link goes here]**

2. Log in with:
   - **Email:** [your admin email]
   - **Password:** [your admin password]

3. You'll see a list of all booking requests from customers, with:
   - Customer name & phone number
   - Device model & the issue they described
   - Their preferred date

4. For each **new (Pending)** request:
   - Click **Approve** if you can take the job — then call the customer directly to confirm timing and pricing
   - Click **Reject** if you can't take it (e.g. fully booked, or you don't service that device)

5. Use the filter buttons (All / Pending / Approved / Rejected) at the top to sort the list.

6. Click **Refresh** to check for new bookings anytime.

---

## Good to know
- New booking requests do **not** send you an automatic phone/SMS alert yet — check the admin panel regularly (e.g. once or twice a day) for new requests.
- The first time you open the site each day, it might take a few extra seconds to load — that's normal, not a problem.
- Payments are **not** handled through the website yet — you'll collect payment directly with the customer (UPI/PhonePe QR is shown on the site) after the repair.
